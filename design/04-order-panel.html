One money column

Quantity on the left, amount on the right, both tabular and lined up in one column. A bartender reads straight down the check. The unit price sits under the name in muted text, so the sums can be checked at a glance.

The price is fixed when the item is rung in

A line keeps the price the item had when it was rung in. If a manager re-prices mid-round, the open check does not change under the bartender, which avoids an argument with a customer about a number that moved.

Sold out mid-order

The line is tinted and labelled rather than deleted, so money cannot disappear from a check without anyone noticing. The bartender removes it on purpose, and the send button stays active because the venue may still honour a round that has already been poured.
