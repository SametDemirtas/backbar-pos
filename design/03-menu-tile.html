The leading edge

One 3px rule down the left of a tile means exactly one thing: someone just changed this item in the manager app. Being in the current order is carried by the raised tile and the gold quantity badge, so the two signals never look alike.

Sold out stays visible

A diagonal hatch across the tile, a struck price and the words SOLD OUT. A colourblind bartender in a dark room still gets all three, and they read from the other end of the bar. The item stays on the menu so the bartender can still answer "have you got any Negroni?"

The change flash

When a manager marks the Espresso Martini sold out, the tile on the till lights up as soon as the snapshot arrives, so the bartender sees the change land.
