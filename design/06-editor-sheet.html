Money is typed as money

A fixed currency mark, tabular figures and a decimal keypad. The value is parsed to integer pence straight away and never becomes a float, because the till has to balance at the end of the night.

Errors say what would happen

Instead of "invalid input", each message says what would go wrong. A manager filling in the form quickly is more likely to read "this will pour free drinks all night".

Delete is easy to find

Outlined rather than filled, at the bottom, and behind a confirmation. It uses ember, the colour the product keeps for what cannot be sold, what cannot be undone, and errors.
